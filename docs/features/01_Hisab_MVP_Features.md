# Hisab — MVP Features

## Product Goal
Bangla-first, Bangladesh-focused business management for small shops and businesses.

## Core MVP
1. Authentication & business setup
2. Dashboard with sales, collections, expenses, profit, receivables, payables and low stock
3. Customer management
4. Customer ledger / বাকি হিসাব
5. Supplier management and supplier ledger
6. Cash and credit sales
7. Purchases
8. Expense management
9. Products and inventory
10. Customer and supplier payments
11. Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket and bank balances
12. Sales and purchase returns
13. PDF invoices/receipts and sharing
14. Daily/weekly/monthly sales, expense, profit, dues and inventory reports
15. Global search
16. Due-payment reminders
17. Notifications
18. Offline-first transaction recording
19. Cloud backup and sync
20. Basic subscription foundation

## MVP AI
### Ask Hisab
Users can ask questions such as:
- এই মাসে সবচেয়ে বেশি বিক্রি কোন পণ্যে?
- কার কাছে সবচেয়ে বেশি টাকা পাব?
- এই মাসে মোট খরচ কত?
- গত মাসের তুলনায় বিক্রি কেমন?

### AI Daily Summary
Generate a concise business summary from actual Hisab data.

### Natural-Language Transaction Entry
Example:
“Rahim 1500 টাকার মাল নিয়েছে, 500 টাকা দিয়েছে।”

Extract customer, sale amount, paid amount and due amount, then show a confirmation screen before saving.

1. Customer ledger
2. Supplier ledger
3. Sales
4. Purchases
5. Expenses
6. Inventory
7. Payments
8. Reports
9. Offline operation
10. Cloud backup

## MVP Success Criteria
A shop owner should be able to open Hisab, record a sale, record payment/due, update stock, see the customer balance and see daily sales/profit in less than a minute for a typical transaction.
