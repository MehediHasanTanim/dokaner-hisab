- (decision) Sync conflict model: financial records are immutable appends and therefore cannot conflict (both devices' entries are kept; the ledger is their sum). Only mutable reference data conflicts, resolved last-writer-wins by server receipt time. Unresolvable cases surface to the owner per FR-97
