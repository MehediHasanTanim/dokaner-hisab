Do
Say the outcome in the owner's words — রহিমের বাকি ৳৪,০৮০ থেকে কমে ৳১,০৮০ হলো — not "Payment recorded".
Make money the largest element on any screen it appears on.
Draw icons literally: a shop, a person, a bag, a book.
Keep every hit target at 48dp, quick actions larger.
Disclose what a number leaves out, in the same breath as the number.
Let colour mean exactly one thing: in, out, warning.
Don't
Never write receivable, payable, debit, credit or ledger in English on a user-facing surface.
Never lead with a chart — the owner wants a number and a sentence.
No dense enterprise tables, no abstract fintech geometry, no gradients.
No emoji as iconography.
Never present an estimate as a fact — লাভ is always আনুমানিক.
Never let an AI surface look like a saved transaction.
